P.O.P.S.Proof of Presence · Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026 · Applies to the P.O.P.S. apps for Android, iOS, Windows, macOS and Linux.

In short: P.O.P.S. is a local-first app. Your case records, evidence, photos, locations and notes are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive your data. We have no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we do not sell or share your information.

1. Information the app stores on your device

Everything you enter or capture is saved in the app's private storage on your device, in a local database and vault folder. This includes case details, contacts and dossiers, calendar entries, court orders, events, members records, reports, and evidence files. Each evidence file gets a SHA-256 fingerprint, and the app keeps a chain-of-custody log and an audit ledger. This information is never sent to us.

3. Network connections

The app works offline. It only connects to the internet when you take one of these actions:

  • Show map on an evidence item loads map images from OpenStreetMap. Their servers receive the requested map area and your IP address, under the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy. Coordinates are shown without the map until you tap Show map.
  • Open in maps, contact research links (Google search and Google News) open in your web browser. Those sites' own privacy policies apply.
  • On desktop, the optional ORB assistant services (local AI model, OCR, voice) run on your own computer at 127.0.0.1 and do not leave it.

4. Moving and sharing your data

Data only leaves your device when you export it yourself: reports, the Full Case Bundle, or a Vault Transfer package (.popsvault). You choose where those files go. A Vault Transfer package holds a full copy of your vault, so store and share it carefully.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app's private storage on your device. Copies you exported yourself are not affected. We cannot recover or delete data for you, because we never have it.

6. Security

Data is protected by your device's app sandbox and device security (screen lock and device encryption where your device supports it). Evidence integrity is protected by SHA-256 fingerprints, re-verification, and a hash-linked audit ledger. Keep your device and any exported files secure.

7. Children

P.O.P.S. is intended for adults managing their own family or legal records. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Your rights

Your information is kept only on your device and under your control, so you can view, correct, export or delete it directly in the app. If you have a privacy question or request under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
